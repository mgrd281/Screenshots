# Snapfly Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 16, 2026*

Snapfly hangs your screenshots on a line at the top of the screen. Everything it
does happens on your Mac.

- **No data is collected.** Snapfly has no account, no analytics, no tracking
  and no advertising. It does not send your screenshots, their text or anything
  about you to the developer or to anyone else.
- **Nothing leaves your Mac.** Reading the text in a screenshot, covering
  private details, translating and describing all run on your Mac, with Apple's
  frameworks.
- **The time stamp.** While Stamp the date and time is on, Snapfly asks the
  atomic clock of the PTB, Germany's national metrology institute
  (ptbtime1.ptb.de), for the exact time of each new screenshot, the way a clock
  sets itself (NTP). Only that question goes out, with nothing about you or your
  screenshots. With the stamp off, Snapfly never connects to the internet.
- **Your files stay yours.** Snapfly reads screenshots in the folders you let it
  watch, and copies, sends or deletes them only when you ask it to.
- **Permissions.** Accessibility access lets Snapfly paste into the app you are
  using when you send a screenshot. Screen Recording lets it take the
  screenshots and recordings you ask for, with ⌃⌥S, the screenshot button or
  Record Screen. So that the send button shows only on chat websites, it asks
  the browser in front for the address of the open page, once you allow it,
  and reads nothing else. While the time stamp is on, that address is also
  written on the stamp of a new screenshot, which stays on your Mac. Settings › Permissions shows each of these, and you
  can turn any off at any time in System Settings.

The full policy, in English, German and Arabic, is at
https://mgrd281.github.io/Screenshots/privacy.html.

## Datenschutz (Deutsch)

Snapfly erhebt keine Daten. Die App hat kein Konto, keine Analyse, kein Tracking
und keine Werbung. Mit dem Internet verbindet sie sich nur, solange der
Zeitstempel eingeschaltet ist, um die Atomuhr der PTB nach der Uhrzeit zu fragen. Texterkennung,
Abdecken privater Angaben, Übersetzen und Beschreiben laufen auf deinem Mac.
Snapfly liest nur Bildschirmfotos in den Ordnern, die du freigibst, und kopiert,
sendet oder löscht sie nur, wenn du es willst. Bildschirmaufnahmen macht es nur,
wenn du darum bittest, und vom Browser liest es nur die Adresse der offenen
Seite, damit die Senden-Taste nur auf Chat-Websites erscheint.
