# What's new in Snapfly

Each version of Snapfly, newest first. Every edition carries the same
number: the download, the Mac App Store and the iPhone. The Releases page
keeps a download of each version.

## 1.1.0

- On the iPhone, scanned pages become one covered PDF with a tap: every
  page covered as Settings says, or as you left it after opening it, a
  picture of each page sized like A4 and no text to copy, ready to send or
  save to Files. Pages show their number, keep their order, open again as
  you left them and can be removed one by one.
- The iPhone app moves with more care: when a picture opens, its covers
  grow over the private details one after another; when the copy is ready
  the green shield bounces once with a light tap; a green line reads the
  page on the Scan tab; and the screenshot cards rise softly into place.
  With Reduce Motion, all of it is quiet.
- On the iPhone, Snapfly is in the share sheet of every app: share a
  picture, choose Snapfly, and it is covered as chosen in the app, checked,
  then copied, shared on or saved, without opening Snapfly.
- The iPhone app has four tabs at the bottom, in Liquid Glass from iOS 26 on:
  Photos, Scan, Protected and More. Scan uses the camera to scan a letter, a
  form or a receipt, straightened and cropped, and covers its private
  details like a screenshot's. Protected shows the screenshots protected
  lately, and More holds Settings and the introduction.
- On the iPhone's home screen, the newest twelve screenshots are small square
  cards, four to a row, with when each was taken, whole, in a small label
  (the time for today's, the date alone for older ones), and Protect a
  Photo is a smaller button.
- Printed forms, like a German sick note, a prescription or a referral, are
  read field by field: the value under or beside each label is covered as
  what the label says it is, and only when that kind is chosen. Names alone
  covers the insured person's name, even on two lines, and the doctor's,
  and leaves the diagnosis, the dates and the insurance numbers readable;
  Health alone covers the diagnosis and the days of illness and leaves the
  names. Labels are never covered, a disease named after a person is no
  name, and an insurance number is no phone number.
- A PDF's covered copy is opened again before it is handed out, and is not
  handed out if it still carries text, notes, form fields or the original's
  author, title, subject or keywords.
- On the iPhone, See All finds screenshots by the words they say (those
  read ahead of time), shows only those with private details, protected or
  not, and puts the newest or the oldest first. It is there whenever there
  are screenshots.
- On the iPhone, Protect Picture puts the picture first: it fills the screen,
  as large as it fits, with the tools, and Copy, Share and Save, as small
  floating icons over it, and how it stands in one small capsule. Details
  goes through the places Snapfly was not sure about one by one, with each
  place shown, Keep Covered, Cover or Leave Visible, and how many are
  checked. Save also saves to Files. Copy, Share or Save tapped while the
  copy is still made goes once it is ready. Full Screen, or a tap beside
  the picture, leaves only the picture; a tap brings the rest back.
- Draw on Photo draws circles: drag round a line, a price or a button and a
  red ring goes round it, as a pen draws one. The O key picks it, and the
  last tool you drew with is ready the next time.
- The iPhone opens on a new home screen: Snapfly's picture of stacked photos
  with a green photo badge, a compact green Protect a Photo button, and the
  newest screenshots in cards that show when each was taken and whether it
  was protected, all of them behind See All; from iOS 18 on, one opens by
  growing out of its card. Holding one covers it and
  copies, shares or saves it. The picture turns small once there are
  screenshots, so they come first. The iPhone's colour is now green.
- A patient's name is covered wherever a report names it: under "Herrn" in
  the address, in the subject, after "Ihren Patienten" in a sentence,
  before a date of birth, under a table's header, with an initial or a
  letter misread. In a doctor's letter, an invoice or a bank statement, a
  line shaped like a name is covered and marked to check, never left as a
  suggestion only.
- Patient names and Other people sit beside Names: Names covers everyone,
  and with it off, only the people chosen are covered. Patient names starts
  on. Whoever chose some people's names before keeps that choice. None of
  them covers a phone number, an address or a QR code.
- On the iPhone, Protect Picture is a workspace: select, the marker, a box
  that turns, the lasso and the eraser under the picture, a tap on a word
  to hide it, handles to move and resize, a double tap to zoom, and Undo
  and Redo for every change. Each place is counted once, protected, to
  check or not protected; Copy, Share and Save ask first while places are
  still to check, and never cut their words short.
- Faster picture processing, off until you turn it on: the iPhone reads
  your pictures from the last 60 days ahead of time, on the iPhone only,
  so they open covered without being read again. Screenshots only, all
  allowed photos, or photos you pick; Pause, Prepare Now and Delete Local
  Index in Settings, with real numbers.
- Names covers people's names and nothing else: a company, a practice or a
  bank, a title, a phone number, an address, an amount or a diagnosis beside
  a name stays readable unless its own kind is chosen.
- Companies and organizations are a kind of their own, and Settings groups
  the kinds in six: People, Companies and organizations, Money, Health,
  Documents, Passwords and access.
- Covering is faster: a screenshot is read once and kept in memory while it
  is worked on, small print is read again side by side, and Apple
  Intelligence is asked only for the kinds chosen.
- On the iPhone, a screenshot shows at once, its private places are marked a
  moment later, and the checked copy follows. Zoom in, tap a place to cover
  or uncover it, draw over a part to cover it, undo and redo, and hold the
  eye to compare. Copy, Share and Save wait until the copy is checked and
  every cover is known to have landed. Protect a Photo opens any picture;
  holding a screenshot offers Cover and Copy; the ones protected lately sit
  in a row of their own.
- Sync between the Mac and the iPhone, through your own iCloud: turn it on in
  Settings on both, and what hangs on the line shows on the iPhone with its
  private details covered, under From your Mac; Send to Mac, under a covered
  screenshot on the iPhone, hangs it on the Mac's line. Originals stay on the
  device unless you choose Originals too. Settings lists your devices and says
  how syncing stands.
- A Mac and an iPhone on different Apple accounts pair with a code: Show
  Code… in Settings › Sync on the Mac shows six digits, a new one every 30
  seconds; type them on the iPhone under Connect to a Mac with a Code, say
  yes on the Mac, and the two sync through iCloud. Disconnect… on either side
  ends it.
- Prompts for AI chats: over ChatGPT, Claude and the other AI chats, numbers
  stand in a small glass bar just above the send button, which keeps its
  size and place. Point at one to see its name;
  click it and the photos go in with that prompt after them. Two come with
  it, Natural writing and Reply to a customer; write up to nine of your own
  in Settings › Line. They are under Ask AI in a photo's menu too.
- Copy Link, in a photo's menu: the photo, covered as it leaves the line, a
  recording or a PDF, behind a link to paste anywhere. The link works for
  seven days, then the file is deleted; Delete Link takes it down sooner.
  The page shows it with Save beside it, in English, German or Arabic.
- The AI assistant has its own pane in Settings, and General is shorter.
  Saving a key checks it with Claude or ChatGPT at once and says whether it
  works; the clipboard button pastes and saves a copied key in one click.
  The model is a menu of the models your key may use, by name, with Other…
  for one typed by hand. The pane lists what the assistant does and holds
  its Find more switch, and an AI card whose key is missing opens it.
- The menu bar menu is tidier: Settings and the screenshots folder at the
  top, then taking, files, the line, the switches, and help. Check for
  Updates… sits just above Quit: the download edition updates itself in one
  click after a question; the App Store edition opens TestFlight or the App
  Store.
- Hide Private Details with Snapfly, from Finder, now sends the covered copy
  straight on: one click on Gmail, WhatsApp, ChatGPT, Claude or any chat on
  for photos, or Copy, or the Mac's Share menu, besides Save Copy…. What to
  hide is chosen in one tidy row of buttons.
- Sound in screen recordings, chosen in Settings › General: the Mac's sound,
  the microphone (macOS 15 and later), or both mixed into one track. None
  until chosen.
- Hide Desktop Icons, in the menu bar: the wallpaper covers the icons on
  every screen for clean screenshots and recordings, and they come back when
  it is turned off.
- Cover Files…, in the menu bar: PDFs and pictures by the hundred, even a
  thousand, dropped or chosen as files or whole folders. Choose what to
  cover, like names alone, and each gets a covered copy in a new folder,
  with the folders they came in kept. A list shows how each went, and those
  with nothing to cover, to look at before sending. A file that cannot be
  covered gets no copy, and the files themselves never change. Several
  files or a folder sent to Hide Private Details with Snapfly in Finder open
  it too.
- Background…, under Edit in a photo's menu: the photo set on a colour, with
  room round it, rounded corners and a soft shadow, for a post, a shop or a
  slide. Eight colours, the room and the corners on sliders, and a size:
  round the photo, square, 16:9, 4:5 or 9:16. It goes out that way, round
  what is covered and drawn; the file stays as it is. Remove Background
  takes it off again.
- Workspace, an option in Settings (off until you turn it on): a folder of
  its own on your Desktop, named after the app, with a folder for each kind
  of screenshot, Orders, Customers, Projects, Documents, Other and Unsorted.
  New screenshots are saved there, safely: written whole or not at all, and
  never over another file. Add, rename, hide, reorder or remove categories,
  choose which one new screenshots go to, rename or move the folder (in
  Settings or in the Finder: Snapfly finds it again), and delete it with a
  question first; everything goes to the Trash. The folder carries Snapfly's
  icon in the Finder. In History, a Workspace page shows them as a grid or a
  list, by day, with search, and each has a menu to open, Quick Look, copy,
  copy its path, show in the Finder, move to another folder, rename or move
  to the Trash. Turning it off, or deleting Snapfly, never deletes a
  screenshot. Not in the Mac App Store edition.
- Record a Guide, in the menu bar: click through what you want to show, and
  each click becomes a step, with a screenshot of the moment, what was
  clicked marked in red, and a sentence like "Click “Orders”." Click the
  capsule above the Dock to finish. In the guide's window, give it a title,
  write any step again, move or delete steps, then Hang on the Line sends it
  as a PDF, or Save makes a PDF or one tall picture. Private details are
  covered in every step, nothing typed is recorded, and the last 20 guides
  are under Recent Guides.
- Hold the arrows on the line's tag a moment, then drag: the line goes
  anywhere, left, right, up or down, and stays there. Near the middle of the
  screen, or the top, it snaps into place. A quick drag still moves it up or
  down only.
- A click on a chat's icon under a photo, or on the send button, sends at
  once. The card of Check before sending, under Privacy, no longer shows
  until you turn it on there.
- A photo's menu is short again: Copy, Copy Text and Paste at the top, then
  Send to (the chats under photos, with More Chats for the rest), Ask AI,
  Text and AI, Edit and Save and Export as submenus, then Open, Quick Look,
  Keep on Top, Show in Finder and Pin, and All Photos and Take down at the
  end. About fifteen rows instead of fifty.
- A cover no longer hides part of the line under or over it: with only the
  name covered on an order page, the street right under the name now reads
  in full.
- The icons in Settings › Line › Icons under photos stand in even rows
  across the whole width: nine and nine, not a full row with two under it.
- Settings › General › AI Assistant looks new: Claude and ChatGPT are two
  tiles with their app's icon, from your Mac, and each says whether a key
  is saved for it. Save sits beside the key, the line under it says where
  the key is kept, and the model's field has a way back to the usual one.
- Convert to SVG…, in a photo's menu, draws the photo again as shapes that
  stay sharp at any size. Your photo and its SVG sit one over the other
  under a divider, and you pick the kind of picture (screenshot, logo or
  icon, illustration, photo, black and white), then colors, smoothness and
  how small a speck to leave out. Copy SVG pastes into Figma, Sketch or
  Illustrator, and Save SVG… writes a file. Straight edges stay straight
  and corners stay sharp. It is made from the copy with the private details
  covered, on your Mac. Soft gradients become bands of flat color.
- Turn on as many icons under photos as you like: six fit under a photo,
  and with more, the last place is ⋯, which lists the rest. In Settings ›
  Line › Icons under photos, a click on an icon turns it on or off.
- Each icon chooses on its own whether it opens in the browser or in its
  app, and Websites open in picks the browser: the Mac's default, or
  another one on it. Each row says where a click goes.
- An AI assistant, Claude or ChatGPT, with your own API key, set up once in
  Settings › General › AI Assistant. Write a Reply, in a photo's menu,
  writes the answer to the customer in the screenshot, ready to send;
  Explain with Claude says what the screenshot shows and answers a question
  about it; Order Details asks it for what your Mac did not find, like what
  was bought. It is sent the screenshot covered, never the original, and
  only when you choose one of these; they bill your account for each
  request. Find more with Claude, under Privacy › Protection, lets it look
  for private details the patterns miss; it is off until you turn it on.
- Invoices and order pages with the labels in one column and the values
  beside them, like Vorname:, Nachname:, Adresse:, PLZ: and Stadt:, have
  those values covered, and the name the bill goes to with its address.
- Smart selection: when you take a screenshot, the picture, card, button
  or paragraph under the pointer is outlined, and a click takes just that.
  Scroll for a bigger or smaller part. A drag keeps to the edges on screen,
  and the empty space round it is cut to an even margin. Crop… does the
  same on a photo. On at first, under Settings › General.
- Twelve more icons under photos, off until you turn them on in Settings ›
  Line › Icons under photos: Perplexity, Grok, DeepSeek, Qwen, Messenger,
  Instagram, Outlook, Yahoo Mail, Facebook, Airbnb, Visual Studio Code and
  Antigravity. A row holds six at a time. WhatsApp has its new logo.
  In Settings every logo shows in its own colours, and the ones under
  photos carry a small check.
- Hide Part covers the word you click, and Always cover, under it, covers
  that word in every photo from then on, as a rule of your own under
  Privacy.
- Rest the pointer on a photo's lock and it shows where it was covered:
  the rest dims, and each covered place is outlined.
- Drag the icons under photos into the order you want, under Line › Icons
  under photos.
- Undo, for a few seconds: a photo taken down or moved to the Trash, a
  crop, or a part hidden by hand, each with a note above the Dock that a
  click takes back.
- An introduction on your own screen, about a minute long: it points at
  the real menu bar icon, your first screenshot, the photo on the line,
  its lock, its icons, its menu and the tag, with the rest of the screen
  dimmed and a small card beside it. Doing a step moves on by itself; it
  can be ended and continued from the menu bar, and Settings › Support ›
  Help & Introduction starts it again, shows one thing at a time, and turns
  off the tips that show once at the right moment. The iPhone has one too.
- Download, on the tag above the trash: the photos picked, or all of them,
  saved into Downloads as they leave the line, covered, drawn on and
  cropped. They stay on the line, and a note shows them in Finder.
- Names finds the customer under a card's heading, like Kunde on a shop's
  order page. Small lines in a colour, read at first as one sign of another
  script, are read again line by line, and a heading with a menu's dots
  beside it, further above its first line, still counts.
- Crop, quickly: choose Crop… in a photo's menu, or ⌥-click the photo, and
  it opens large. Drag over the part to keep, and it is kept as you let go;
  Return keeps the whole photo. It goes out cropped, and the file stays as
  it is.
- ChatGPT, Claude, WhatsApp, Gmail, GMX and WEB.DE in a row under a photo,
  so the photo itself stays calm, each with its own switch under Line ›
  Icons under photos.
  WhatsApp, Gmail, GMX and WEB.DE show their logos; ChatGPT and Claude show
  the app's own icon from your Mac. A click opens the website in your
  browser; Open in, under Line, brings the app back. GMX and WEB.DE open the
  mailbox and copy the covered photo for you to paste into a new message.
- The menu bar icon says how many photos hang, and shows a small dot while
  something new hangs that the line has not shown yet. Count on the menu
  bar icon, under Line, turns it off.
- One quiet line at the top of Settings › Privacy: how many details
  Snapfly covered in the photos you sent in the last 7 days.
- Sent, and a way back: once sent photos leave the line, a note above the
  Dock says where they went, and a click on it hangs them again.
- Not during a call, under Line: while the microphone is in use, in Zoom,
  Teams, FaceTime or a call in the browser, the line stays up unless you
  bring it down, the send button over chats stays away, and nothing of
  Snapfly shows in what you share.
- Questions for ChatGPT and Claude, under Line: right-click ChatGPT or
  Claude on a photo, or choose Ask ChatGPT in its menu, and the photo goes
  with a question after it, like "Summarize this in a few lines". Write
  your own in Settings.
- Six looks for covers, under Privacy: ink, labelled (the ink says what it
  hides, like Name or Phone), blur, pixels, dots and erased. Each hides
  for good: the soft ones are drawn from the colors around what is covered,
  never from it, so nothing can be brought back.
- Keep the last four digits, under Privacy: card, account and phone numbers
  stay recognisable, as on a bank statement.
- Every copy is checked before it leaves: read again as anyone could read
  it, with whatever private still shows covered too.
- Find more with Apple Intelligence, under Privacy, on a Mac or iPhone that
  has it: the model on the device covers what the patterns miss, like a
  name in a sentence. Nothing leaves the device.
- Strongest protection, under Privacy: every line with something private is
  covered whole, with more room around it.
- Two new kinds to cover: API keys and tokens (on from the start), and IP
  addresses. Crypto wallets count as bank accounts, a map's coordinates as
  an address, and Wi-Fi passwords, licence keys and booking codes as codes.
  More labels are known, like a contract, member or insurance number.
- Every edit says where it went: after Markup or Trim, a note names the
  folder the photo was saved in, and a click on it shows the photo in
  Finder. Done in Draw on Photo and Hide Part says the edit is kept with
  the photo, and the file stays as it is.
- Save Copy…, in a photo's menu and in Draw on Photo, keeps the photo as it
  goes out, drawn on, cropped or covered, as a file of its own.
- Pick photos with the circle on each, opposite the cross, to send just
  those. Picked photos stay picked when the line goes up, so the send
  button over a chat sends them alone. Pick All and Unpick All are in a
  photo's menu.
- ChatGPT and Claude beside the paper plane: a click sends the photo, or
  the ones picked with it, straight into the app, on the chat it shows or a
  new one. Their own icons show while the apps are on the Mac; a photo's
  menu also opens the website.
- The cross and the paper plane on a photo now answer where they are drawn
  in right-to-left languages.
- Draw on Photo, in a photo's menu: arrows, boxes, a highlighter, numbered
  steps and words, and a crop to the part that matters, drawn on what
  leaves the line. Each tool has its key, and ⌘Z undoes. The file itself
  stays as it is.
- Cover what lands in a folder, under Privacy: every picture or PDF that
  lands in a folder you choose gets a covered copy in another, in the
  background, with one note when a run is done.
- Test on a Sample, under Privacy: a made-up order covered with what you
  checked, to see what each kind hides.
- Send with Shop Header, in a photo's menu: the covered screenshot goes
  under a band with your shop's name and logo, the order and the day. Set
  them under Line in Settings.
- Monthly pack, under Bookkeeping in Settings: a month's receipts in one
  PDF for the tax advisor, a page listing them with the sum of the totals,
  then each receipt on a page of its own.
- Pictures leave as fresh files: what they show, without the notes a file
  carries on when, where and with what it was made, or where it was
  downloaded from. Plain file names, under Privacy, call them "Image" too.
- Cover everything in AI chats, under Privacy: whatever goes into ChatGPT,
  Claude, Gemini or another AI chat has every kind covered, checked or not.
  The send button over the chat shows a lock by the count meanwhile.
- Check before sending, under Privacy: before photos go into an AI chat, or
  an app or website not known as a chat, a card shows them as they will go
  and what is covered. Return sends them, Escape keeps them on the line.
- Save for Bookkeeping and Email to Bookkeeping…, in a photo's menu: a
  covered copy named for its day, order and total, with a line in
  Snapfly.csv, or in a new email to whoever keeps the books.
- Saved messages, under Line in Settings, wait above the message bar.
  {order}, {total}, {date}, {name} and {tracking} fill in from the photo; a
  blank of a covered kind stays empty.
- Order Details, in a photo's menu: the order number, date, total, name,
  address, email, phone and tracking number read off a screenshot, each
  ready to copy, or all of them as a row for a spreadsheet.
- Order Details reads a GiroCode or a Swiss QR bill as a transfer: the
  account, who is paid, the amount and the reference.
- Orders, in the History window: photos with an order, invoice or customer
  number together under that number.
- Sent, in the History window: what went into which chat, when, with what
  covered and the message, to search or export as a table. The History
  keeps photos 90 days, a year or forever too.
- The message bar keeps the last five messages: click one, or press ↑ and
  ↓, to send it again.
- The message bar sends in another language: choose it with the globe, and
  Return translates your message on your Mac, to read before the next
  Return sends it. Kept for each chat, on macOS 15 and later.
- Send Again in the History: a photo sent before goes into the chat in
  front once more, covered.
- Keep on Top, in a photo's menu: it floats over every app in a small
  window, to read from while you write in a chat.
- Snapfly has version numbers of its own now, and this list says what each
  one brings. Settings › About shows yours.
- QR codes and barcodes are covered, a new kind under Privacy, on the Mac
  and the iPhone. Nobody can read what a code holds by looking, so it is
  covered whole, with the number printed under a barcode. It starts on for
  everyone who covers anything.
- The lock on a photo says what was covered: rest the pointer on it, or open
  the photo's menu, for "Covered: Names · QR codes and barcodes". VoiceOver
  says it too, and the iPhone shows it under the covered picture. The lock
  now shows only when something was covered.
- A paper plane after the lock marks a photo that says what one sent lately
  said. Its menu says where that went and when, and so does Check before
  sending. It needs the History, and turns off under Line in Settings.
- On the iPhone: rules of your own under Also hide, as on the Mac, and
  Check All, Recommended and Uncheck All in Settings.

## 1.0.0

Everything up to October 2026, under one number:

- The line: screenshots hang just above the screen, out of sight until the
  pointer rests in the menu bar. A click copies, a drag shares, a paper
  plane sends into the chat in front.
- Screenshots and screen recordings taken by Snapfly itself, the screenshot
  button, whole pages scrolled from top to bottom, and GIFs of recordings.
- Private details covered in whatever leaves the line, in photos and PDFs
  too: names, addresses, bank and health details, order numbers and more,
  plus your own rules and Hide Part.
- The time stamp from the PTB's atomic clock, the History, Translate Text
  and Describe, eight line styles, and Snapfly for the iPhone.
