# What's new in Snapfly

Each version of Snapfly, newest first. Every edition carries the same
number: the download, the Mac App Store and the iPhone. The Releases page
keeps a download of each version.

## 1.1.0

- ChatGPT, Claude, WhatsApp, Gmail, GMX and WEB.DE in a row under a photo,
  so the photo itself stays calm, each with its own switch under Line.
  WhatsApp, Gmail, GMX and WEB.DE show their logos; ChatGPT and Claude show
  the app's own icon from your Mac. A click opens the website in your
  browser; Open in, under Line, brings the app back. GMX and WEB.DE open the
  mailbox and copy the covered photo for you to paste into a new message.
- The menu bar icon says how many photos hang, and shows a small dot while
  something new hangs that the line has not shown yet. Count on the menu
  bar icon, under Line, turns it off.
- One quiet line at the top of Settings › Privacy: how many details
  Snapfly covered in the photos you sent in the last 7 days.
- Sent, and a way back: once sent photos leave the line, a note above the
  Dock says where they went, and a click on it hangs them again.
- Not during a call, under Line: while the microphone is in use, in Zoom,
  Teams, FaceTime or a call in the browser, the line stays up unless you
  bring it down, the send button over chats stays away, and nothing of
  Snapfly shows in what you share.
- Questions for ChatGPT and Claude, under Line: right-click ChatGPT or
  Claude on a photo, or choose Ask ChatGPT in its menu, and the photo goes
  with a question after it, like "Summarize this in a few lines". Write
  your own in Settings.
- Six looks for covers, under Privacy: ink, labelled (the ink says what it
  hides, like Name or Phone), blur, pixels, dots and erased. Each hides
  for good: the soft ones are drawn from the colors around what is covered,
  never from it, so nothing can be brought back.
- Keep the last four digits, under Privacy: card, account and phone numbers
  stay recognisable, as on a bank statement.
- Every copy is checked before it leaves: read again as anyone could read
  it, with whatever private still shows covered too.
- Find more with Apple Intelligence, under Privacy, on a Mac or iPhone that
  has it: the model on the device covers what the patterns miss, like a
  name in a sentence. Nothing leaves the device.
- Strongest protection, under Privacy: every line with something private is
  covered whole, with more room around it.
- Two new kinds to cover: API keys and tokens (on from the start), and IP
  addresses. Crypto wallets count as bank accounts, a map's coordinates as
  an address, and Wi-Fi passwords, licence keys and booking codes as codes.
  More labels are known, like a contract, member or insurance number.
- Every edit says where it went: after Markup or Trim, a note names the
  folder the photo was saved in, and a click on it shows the photo in
  Finder. Done in Draw on Photo and Hide Part says the edit is kept with
  the photo, and the file stays as it is.
- Save Copy…, in a photo's menu and in Draw on Photo, keeps the photo as it
  goes out, drawn on, cropped or covered, as a file of its own.
- Pick photos with the circle on each, opposite the cross, to send just
  those. Picked photos stay picked when the line goes up, so the send
  button over a chat sends them alone. Pick All and Unpick All are in a
  photo's menu.
- ChatGPT and Claude beside the paper plane: a click sends the photo, or
  the ones picked with it, straight into the app, on the chat it shows or a
  new one. Their own icons show while the apps are on the Mac; a photo's
  menu also opens the website.
- The cross and the paper plane on a photo now answer where they are drawn
  in right-to-left languages.
- Draw on Photo, in a photo's menu: arrows, boxes, a highlighter, numbered
  steps and words, and a crop to the part that matters, drawn on what
  leaves the line. Each tool has its key, and ⌘Z undoes. The file itself
  stays as it is.
- Cover what lands in a folder, under Privacy: every picture or PDF that
  lands in a folder you choose gets a covered copy in another, in the
  background, with one note when a run is done.
- Test on a Sample, under Privacy: a made-up order covered with what you
  checked, to see what each kind hides.
- Send with Shop Header, in a photo's menu: the covered screenshot goes
  under a band with your shop's name and logo, the order and the day. Set
  them under Line in Settings.
- Monthly pack, under Bookkeeping in Settings: a month's receipts in one
  PDF for the tax advisor, a page listing them with the sum of the totals,
  then each receipt on a page of its own.
- Pictures leave as fresh files: what they show, without the notes a file
  carries on when, where and with what it was made, or where it was
  downloaded from. Plain file names, under Privacy, call them "Image" too.
- Cover everything in AI chats, under Privacy: whatever goes into ChatGPT,
  Claude, Gemini or another AI chat has every kind covered, checked or not.
  The send button over the chat shows a lock by the count meanwhile.
- Check before sending, under Privacy: before photos go into an AI chat, or
  an app or website not known as a chat, a card shows them as they will go
  and what is covered. Return sends them, Escape keeps them on the line.
- Save for Bookkeeping and Email to Bookkeeping…, in a photo's menu: a
  covered copy named for its day, order and total, with a line in
  Snapfly.csv, or in a new email to whoever keeps the books.
- Saved messages, under Line in Settings, wait above the message bar.
  {order}, {total}, {date}, {name} and {tracking} fill in from the photo; a
  blank of a covered kind stays empty.
- Order Details, in a photo's menu: the order number, date, total, name,
  address, email, phone and tracking number read off a screenshot, each
  ready to copy, or all of them as a row for a spreadsheet.
- Order Details reads a GiroCode or a Swiss QR bill as a transfer: the
  account, who is paid, the amount and the reference.
- Orders, in the History window: photos with an order, invoice or customer
  number together under that number.
- Sent, in the History window: what went into which chat, when, with what
  covered and the message, to search or export as a table. The History
  keeps photos 90 days, a year or forever too.
- The message bar keeps the last five messages: click one, or press ↑ and
  ↓, to send it again.
- The message bar sends in another language: choose it with the globe, and
  Return translates your message on your Mac, to read before the next
  Return sends it. Kept for each chat, on macOS 15 and later.
- Send Again in the History: a photo sent before goes into the chat in
  front once more, covered.
- Keep on Top, in a photo's menu: it floats over every app in a small
  window, to read from while you write in a chat.
- Snapfly has version numbers of its own now, and this list says what each
  one brings. Settings › About shows yours.
- QR codes and barcodes are covered, a new kind under Privacy, on the Mac
  and the iPhone. Nobody can read what a code holds by looking, so it is
  covered whole, with the number printed under a barcode. It starts on for
  everyone who covers anything.
- The lock on a photo says what was covered: rest the pointer on it, or open
  the photo's menu, for "Covered: Names · QR codes and barcodes". VoiceOver
  says it too, and the iPhone shows it under the covered picture. The lock
  now shows only when something was covered.
- A paper plane after the lock marks a photo that says what one sent lately
  said. Its menu says where that went and when, and so does Check before
  sending. It needs the History, and turns off under Line in Settings.
- On the iPhone: rules of your own under Also hide, as on the Mac, and
  Check All, Recommended and Uncheck All in Settings.

## 1.0.0

Everything up to October 2026, under one number:

- The line: screenshots hang just above the screen, out of sight until the
  pointer rests in the menu bar. A click copies, a drag shares, a paper
  plane sends into the chat in front.
- Screenshots and screen recordings taken by Snapfly itself, the screenshot
  button, whole pages scrolled from top to bottom, and GIFs of recordings.
- Private details covered in whatever leaves the line, in photos and PDFs
  too: names, addresses, bank and health details, order numbers and more,
  plus your own rules and Hide Part.
- The time stamp from the PTB's atomic clock, the History, Translate Text
  and Describe, eight line styles, and Snapfly for the iPhone.
