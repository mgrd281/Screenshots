# What's new in Snapfly

Each version of Snapfly, newest first. Every edition carries the same
number: the download, the Mac App Store and the iPhone. The Releases page
keeps a download of each version.

## 1.1.0

- On the iPhone, a scanned page has a fourth colour, Originalfarbe: the
  paper keeps its own colour, so a pink sick note stays pink, evenly lit
  and straightened, the print as dark as in Farbe.

- On the iPhone, scanned pages can be kept: Unter Zuletzt behalten puts
  them among your recent documents, on this iPhone only.

- On the iPhone, the Scan tab starts a scan in one tap: one green card,
  three quick actions (Aus Fotos, Zusammenfügen, PDF öffnen) and your
  recent documents, each shown as its first page with the private details
  covered. How it works is behind the "?", and scrolled down, a green
  Scannen button waits in the bar.

- On the iPhone, a test in More › Detection, Schärfere kleine Schrift
  (Test), scans with Snapfly's own camera: photos at full size, the page
  taken by itself once small print is sharp and the phone still, and one
  short hint at a time otherwise (Ruhig halten, Näher ran, Spiegelung, Handy
  leicht kippen, Mehr Licht, Linse reinigen). Off at first. With it,
  Näher aufnehmen takes only the small print again, close up, and lays it
  onto the page where it then reads more surely.

- On the iPhone, Review says "Kleine Schrift unscharf" where a page's
  smallest print reads unsurely, with Näher aufnehmen to take the page again
  with the camera, or Neu aufnehmen für kleine Schrift for a photo.

- On the iPhone, small light-grey print beside a page's dark text comes out
  of Scan as dark and plain as the rest, line by line, with every umlaut's
  dot, accent, comma and @ kept, where cleaning used to leave "Arzte fur
  Radiologie". A thin light face comes out a little bolder, so it reads as
  dark as the body. Each page is read again after cleaning: a line of that
  grey print that reads otherwise, a dot or an accent gone, is read on its own, cleaned
  again gently, or kept as the light alone leaves it, whichever first reads
  as before. A dot the photo left too faint to read before and cleaning
  made readable counts as clearer, not different.

- On the iPhone, Review says how many private details your categories leave
  visible ("Nichts verdeckt · 3 sichtbar") and lists them under Sichtbar
  gelassen, each to cover with a tap, with a word where no name of yours is
  kept in Always Hide on that iPhone. It never says "Nichts Privates" while
  they show.

- On the iPhone, Review is new: one job, checking the covers. A chip under
  the title says what is covered and what is still to check, and opens one
  list of every page's places, each with Lassen and Verdecken, Alle
  verdecken, the pages' hints and what is covered. The page is much larger,
  the places to check outlined in orange, with an eye in its corner to see
  it without covers and a swipe sideways to the next page. Under it, the
  pages with a small orange dot where something is open, and one bar of
  tools: Abdecken or Seite, with Löschen last and in red. Weiter is the one
  green button; sending to apps is in Sichern und teilen, and the menu holds
  KI fragen, Alle Seiten, Neu aufnehmen and Scan verwerfen.

- On the iPhone, the name you keep in Always Hide is covered on every page
  even where the page's reading gets it a little wrong: surname first with
  a comma, broken over two lines, in capitals, with its space left out or
  put in, or with a letter misread, as an l for an i, a 1 or a 0 for a
  letter, or rn for an m. It is also found again with what you added to
  Always Hide since the page was opened. The line under Review never says
  "Nichts Privates gefunden" while a place is still to check or the page
  has a fault: it says "3 verdeckt · 4 zu prüfen", "3 verdeckt, alles
  geprüft" or "Nichts Privates · 1 Qualitätshinweis".

- On the iPhone, Review says so when the page's text could not be read,
  with Try Again, instead of finding nothing private on it; and your name
  is covered where it runs on into the next line of its column, even with
  another column's line read between. Review's bar is tidier: the title in
  the middle with room to the menu, a smaller Weiter, Undo and Redo as
  icons alone so every tool's name shows on the smallest iPhone, the eye in
  the page's bottom corner, and in the list a long kind cut short with
  "…", its buttons under it at the largest text sizes.

- On the iPhone, a phone photo of a letter comes out of Scan with its lines
  straight, never bent into a wave by a row of text taken for a ruled line,
  and its small light-grey print, an address or telephone numbers in a
  margin, comes out plain to read instead of faint and broken.

- On the iPhone, Scan cleans a real photo of a paper better, still only by
  making pixels lighter or darker and moving them, never by redrawing or
  retyping anything. A fold or a soft shadow from a hand or the phone is
  evened out like the rest of the paper, while a printed grey box or a
  photo keeps its grey: Snapfly tells them apart by their edges, soft for
  light, sharp for print. A folded sheet is made flat part by part: where
  both its edges bend at a fold, each part gets its own perspective and the
  parts are joined so lines go on without a step. Pink, yellow or blue paper
  turns white while a blue signature or a red stamp keeps its colour. Light
  print, as from a typewriter or a carbon copy, comes out dark. And the
  edges are cut in until nothing of the table or floor is left. A page from
  a flatbed scanner comes out as clean: the thin crease where it was folded
  is evened out like a shadow, while a pale watermark or a shaded row keeps
  the tint it was printed in, even where it reaches the sheet's edge,
  instead of going white in places. The orange note that less text reads
  after cleaning no longer comes for a typewriter's Z or O read once as 2
  or 0, and the part of a folded sheet below a fold is laid true where a
  hand's shadow hid one end of the fold.

- On the iPhone, PDF projects: put photos, scans, screenshots and PDFs
  together into one PDF, or open a PDF to put its pages in order and cover
  them. Under the scan card, "PDF erstellen und bearbeiten" offers Merge
  and Open PDF, and Recently Edited goes on where you left off; a PDF or a
  picture opened in Snapfly from Files, Mail or Safari starts a project, and
  the Protected tab merges the screenshots you choose. The overview shows
  the pages three to a row in their own shape, with where each comes from;
  hold a page and drag it to its place while the others make room; choose
  pages to turn, duplicate, save as a PDF of their own or delete, and undo
  or redo each step from the note that says what was done. The pages open
  in Protect Picture, a swipe up or down to the next or the one before,
  zoom up to eight times, with their covers found from the PDF's own text.
  A PDF's password is asked each time and never kept, and a signed PDF
  says that editing ends its signature. Save and Share sets the page size
  for pictures (Original, A4, US Letter), the quality with the file's size,
  covers and searchable text. The files you add are never changed; the
  project is kept on the iPhone, protected and out of backups, and opens
  again where you left it.

- On the iPhone, Scan cleans each page as a good scanner does, from the
  camera or from photos you choose in Photos: the page is found and cut
  from the photo (its corners set by hand, with a loupe, where Snapfly is
  not sure), made flat in its own shape, turned upright and levelled, its
  light made even, the paper white and the print dark and sharp. Only the
  picture as a whole changes: nothing is drawn, filled in, taken away or
  guessed. Each page is read before and after, and where a number is no
  longer read, the text is blurred, a reflection hides it, the page is
  small in the picture or the lens looks smudged, an orange card says where,
  with Take Again; then the covers are found on the cleaned page. In
  Review, the cleaned page wipes in over the photo and its covers sweep in;
  Compare shows Before and After either side of a divider; the document's
  pages sit in a strip, to reorder, add or delete; and Color, Grayscale or
  Black and White changes every page. Save and Share makes one A4 PDF,
  whose text you can find and copy without what is covered, or a PNG for
  each page, with covers, where every save starts, or without, which says
  the file holds every private detail.
- On the iPhone, a photo chosen with Protect a Photo that looks like a
  photographed paper document, never a screenshot, offers to straighten and
  brighten it as a scan is: Clean Up does, its covers are found again on
  the cleaned page, and Revert brings back the photo as it was. Nothing is
  cleaned without the tap.
- On the iPhone, Scan names the document in Save and Share, a name you can
  change: with Apple Intelligence on, from the words outside the covers on
  the first page, its kind, who sent it and its date, never a covered word
  or your own name; otherwise by when it was scanned ("Scan 21.10.2026,
  14.05"). The document's kind, Invoice, Letter, Form or Receipt, shows
  beside its pages.
- On the iPhone, a tap on ChatGPT, Claude or another AI chat for scanned
  pages says the covered copy goes to it, never the original, and offers
  three questions to send with it: Explain the Document, Summarize, and
  List Deadlines and Amounts, each asking never to guess what is covered or
  unreadable and never to fill in or correct a number. The conversation is
  in your own app; Snapfly holds no key for it and sends nothing itself.
- On the iPhone, a covered picture saved as a PNG leaves without the Exif
  note the system writes into it.
- On the iPhone, the question before a picture leaves says "1 place still
  to check" for one, not "1 places".
- Studio, for screen recordings on the Mac. A recording opens in it once it
  hangs, already zoomed in where you clicked and typed, following the
  pointer there, with a ring at each click. Draw arrows, boxes, circles,
  numbered steps and words over it, each for as long as you want, and move
  or stretch zooms and marks on the timeline, under pictures of the
  recording and its sound. Save Video makes a smooth new video at sixty
  frames a second beside the recording, with its sound. Edit › Edit in
  Studio… opens it later; Settings › General can stop it opening after each
  recording.
- On the iPhone, More is a short overview now, with a page for each part.
  A card says how many of the twenty-two kinds are covered in every
  picture, with a little document covered in your look, and turns grey,
  Protection Off, when none is. Four tiles open What Is Covered, Always
  Hide, Cover Style and Detection, each showing what it holds. What Is
  Covered shows what it does: a made-up doctor's bill, chat and note in
  which every kind you choose is covered as you choose it, in your look.
  Six tiles open each group's switches in a short sheet over the document,
  which turns to where the kind shows and sweeps its cover over it, and the
  "…" menu sets the recommended choice, everything or nothing at once.
  Cover Style shows a bill covered in the look you choose, as
  the two switches have it. Detection holds Apple Intelligence, the check
  every copy gets before it leaves, and how fast covering is on your
  iPhone. AI Assistant, Send to Apps, the double tap on the back, and Mac
  and iCloud sit under Features, the introduction and the demo under Help.
  Every setting keeps the value it had. The tab bar no longer shrinks to a
  round button over the rows on More, and every icon there is in the one
  green.
- On the iPhone, Also hide is now Always Hide, under More, and
  shows at a glance what is covered in every picture, with one quiet line
  on what keeps it private. Your details stand as rows with their kind's
  icon, a card or an IBAN by its last four digits alone. Your rules have a
  switch each and say how many are on. Its sheets are just as tall as what
  they hold and sit whole above the keyboard: a new detail is chosen from
  a list of the four kinds, a name typed as first and last name; a new
  rule is a word or comes after a label, with one line of an example
  covered as you type. A label covers what follows it, or the line below
  when nothing does, as before, or always the same line or always the next
  line. Edit reorders and deletes, and a swipe deletes too. Empty rules
  that Settings once kept, shown as rules without a word, are gone, and an
  empty rule can no longer be saved.
- On the Mac, your details and rules have a pane of their own in
  Settings, Always Hide, as on the iPhone: each detail with its kind's
  colour, a card or an IBAN by its last four digits; each rule with what
  it covers and a switch, and how many are on. A name is typed as first
  and last name; a rule's sheet covers an example as you type and lets a
  label keep to the same line or the next line.
- Your details and rules are covered for sure, never marked to check. A
  name, a card's number or an IBAN broken over two lines is covered, and
  so is one with a letter misread; what your rules cover is hidden from the
  words sent to the AI assistant too. A card is kept only once its number
  adds up, on the Mac as well. Rules kept before stay on and work as they
  did.
- Your own details under Also hide, on the Mac and the iPhone: your name,
  your address, your bank cards and your bank accounts. Each is covered
  in the ways it is written, whatever is checked: a name surname first, in
  capitals or with an initial; a street with its house number and the
  postcode with the town; a card's whole number and "•••• 4242" where only
  its last digits show; an IBAN with or without spaces, and a German
  account's number. A card's number is grouped and its network named as you
  type it, an IBAN checked. They stay in the keychain of that device alone,
  never in iCloud, and in an iPhone's backup only sealed to that iPhone;
  they are hidden from the words sent to the AI assistant. A name saved as
  a word moves there by itself.
- On the iPhone, the Protected tab clears up easily. Hold a screenshot and
  tap Remove from Protected: it leaves the list at once, with Undo for a
  few seconds, and stays in Photos. Or tap Delete from Photos, which iOS
  asks about first. Select, then a tap on each or a finger drawn across,
  takes several off at once.
- On the iPhone, ChatGPT, Claude and the other AI chats open straight away
  with the protected copy ready to paste: tap the picture above the
  keyboard there, or hold the message field and tap Paste. Claude opens a
  new chat. Where the app is not on the iPhone, the share sheet shows as
  before. Save to Photos opens Photos once the copy is saved.
- Names, Patient names and Other people are three switches of their own,
  on the Mac and the iPhone, and each covers only its own people: Patient
  names the patients'; Other people the doctors, customers, staff and
  everyone else the words round a name tell; Names every name whose role
  nothing tells, as in a chat or a list, and the one who signs a letter
  that is no doctor's. With Names on alone, a doctor's letter about you
  covers your name and leaves the doctors'. Your name, saved as a word, is
  covered however it is written with any of the three on. All three, on at
  first, cover everyone's names, and a choice of Names with Patient names
  covers as before, with Other people turned on; so does Cover Files' last
  choice, and the share extension's until Snapfly is opened. Cover Files and
  Hide Part show each switch's subtitle, as Settings does. "Herr" is no
  longer covered on its own, as in "Sehr geehrter Herr Kollege", and a
  doctor a practice lists between two others in its letterhead without
  "Dr. med." is known as a doctor, not marked to check.
- On the iPhone, People in Settings opens with Your Name: your name with
  "always covered", or a row to add it. A name saved as a word moves under
  Your Details as Snapfly starts.
- Only the kinds you checked under Privacy are covered, on the Mac and the
  iPhone. Apple Intelligence, or Claude or ChatGPT, no longer covers a
  street, a date, a reference or a medical term it calls a name: what it
  names is read again by Snapfly's own rules, and what may fit is offered
  for you to check. A street is covered with its house number. A heading in
  capitals, like "WEITERER STANDORT", is no longer taken for a name, and a
  name over a letter's address or beside a date of birth is sure, not "to
  check". Your own name, saved as a word under Also hide, is known however
  it is written.
- On the iPhone, Protect Picture is calmer and the page leads: it stands
  on a bright ground with a soft shadow, as large as the room allows. Under
  the title a small capsule says how many private details were found, like
  "3 sensible Angaben erkannt", and how many are still to check. The apps
