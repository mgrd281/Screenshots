Snapfly covers the private details in your screenshots, photos and documents before you share them. Reading and covering happen on your iPhone.

PROTECT A PHOTO
Open a screenshot or a photo and Snapfly covers names, addresses, emails, phone numbers, bank details, amounts, health details, ID numbers and more. Places it is not sure about wait for you to review: Keep Covered or Leave Visible. Cover more by hand with the marker, a box or the lasso. Pick a Cover Style, keep the last four digits, or turn on Strongest protection.

ALWAYS HIDE
Add your name, address, cards and accounts, and words or labels of your own. Snapfly covers them in every picture.

SCAN DOCUMENTS
Scan a letter, an invoice or a form with the camera, or take pages from photos. Snapfly straightens and cleans each page, covers the private details, and lets you check every cover in Review. Save and Share makes a PDF, a picture of each page, text or a Word document, covered.
- Show Text: copy, search and translate a scan's text, or hear it with Read Aloud. Nothing under a cover is shown or read.
- Add Payment Reminder: for an invoice still to pay, the Calendar's own form opens filled in. Snapfly never sees your calendar.
- All Documents searches the words in every document.
- PDF projects put photos, scans, screenshots and PDFs into one file, to order and cover.

SEND ONLY THE PROTECTED COPY
With one tap, send to ChatGPT, Claude, WhatsApp, Gmail and other apps, or to Photos and Files. The protected copy goes, never the original.

SHORTCUTS AND BACK TAP
Cover the Latest Screenshot covers your newest screenshot and copies it, ready to paste. Set it to a double tap on the back of your iPhone. Snapfly is in every app's share sheet too.

WITH YOUR MAC
Turn on Sync and Snapfly for Mac keeps in step through your own iCloud. Only covered copies go, unless you choose originals too.

No account. No tracking.
