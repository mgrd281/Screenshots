# Brand Context

A short summary for the brand skills in `.claude/skills/` (brand-identity,
brand-guidelines, brand-audit, brand-voice), which read this file first.
It is not the source of truth: `BRAND_IDENTITY.md`, `BRAND_GUIDELINES.md`,
`DESIGN.md` and `PRODUCT.md` are, and they win wherever this file differs.
Update it when they change. The brand exists; these skills document and
check it, they don't rename or rebrand it.

## Brand
- **Name**: Snapfly
- **Category**: macOS utility (menu bar app for screenshots)
- **Description**: Screenshots on a line at the top of the screen, one click from any chat.
- **Stage**: Early. Free direct download from GitHub Releases; a Mac App Store edition is being prepared (uploads to TestFlight are set up).
- **Website**: https://mgrd281.github.io/Screenshots/ (English), /de/ and /ar/

## Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Mac users who send screenshots into chats every day, to people or to ChatGPT, often of orders and invoices. The owner, working in German and Arabic, is the first of them.
- **Key Problem**: Getting the right screenshot, with private details covered, into the right chat in as few moves as possible, without screenshots piling up on the Desktop.
- **Their Language**: Not recorded. Use the app's own words (`Localizable.strings`), not invented quotes.

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: The line itself, a physical metaphor above every app; built for sending into a chat (a paper plane on each photo, ⌃⌥V); private details covered before anything leaves; nothing leaves the Mac.
- **Competitors**: Not recorded by the owner. The everyday alternative is macOS's own screenshot tool, which saves to the Desktop.
- **Market Position**: Free, native, privacy-first utility.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: quiet, precise, native, private, quick
- **Tone**: Casual and plain, calm, never hyped. German addresses the reader as "du".
- **Voice Admires**: Apple's own software (as a standard of craft, not to copy).

## Values & Mission
- **Core Values**: Sending first; never take focus from the app in front; private by default; quiet until needed; one feature, every style.
- **Mission**: The shortest path from a screenshot to a chat, with nothing private leaving the Mac.

## Goals
- **Primary Goal**: Not recorded. Current work is the Mac App Store listing.
- **Key Metrics**: Not recorded. Never invent numbers, testimonials or benchmarks.
